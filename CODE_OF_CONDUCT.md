# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free project for everyone.

Be constructive, assume good intent, focus criticism on the work, and respect privacy. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, or sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to `gpt123@panw3i.com`. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
